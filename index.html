The idea

Four apps on a music stand is four apps nobody keeps a record in.

Rehearsio is built around one thing: the practice session. You start it, and everything else happens inside — the score is open, the metronome is under your hand, the recorder is running, the time counts itself. What's left afterwards is a record, not a memory.

  1. Start

    One press. The clock runs and the session is open behind everything else.

  2. Play

    Score on screen, metronome a tap away, the recording already going.

  3. Mark

    Pencil on the page, a note to yourself, a task for tomorrow.

  4. Look back

    How long, on what, at what tempo — and how it actually sounded.

What's inside

Nine tools that already know about each other.

  • Sheet music library

    Bring in PDFs from Files, Mail or anywhere that can share. They open where you left off.

  • Pencil marks

    Six pencils and a highlighter, straight onto the page. Fingerings, breaths, the bar you keep missing.

  • Metronome

    Time signatures, accents, subdivisions. It keeps its beat while the rest of the app is busy.

  • Tuner

    Note, cents and a needle that holds still. Works for anything with a pitch.

  • Recording

    Record the run-through and hear it back. The recording stays on the phone unless you send it somewhere.

  • Notes

    What the teacher said, what to fix, what to ask next time. Searchable, tagged, tied to the piece.

  • Stopwatch and timer

    How long you actually played, split by what you played. Counted for you, not by you.

  • Progress

    Days in a row, minutes per piece, tempo over weeks. A calendar you can read at a glance.

  • Backup you own

    Your whole library in one file — scores, marks, recordings, history. Put it wherever you keep things.

The app

Built to be read from a music stand.

Big figures, warm paper-coloured surfaces, and no colour of its own — so the only bright things on screen are the marks you made.
